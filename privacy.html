Privacy Policy

Holiday Hacker · Last updated: 30 April 2026

Short version: Holiday Hacker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you create — trips, reminders, favorites, preferences — stays on your device, in the app's local storage. There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no backend server.

1. What data we collect

None on our servers. Holiday Hacker has no server. We do not run a backend that receives or stores user data. The following data is created by you on your own device and is stored only there:

  • Trip plans, holiday windows, and saved destinations.
  • Reminder preferences (days before trip, reminder time).
  • Travel mode selection (train / bus / flight / car) per trip.
  • Favorites and travel preferences.

This data lives in localStorage on the device and never leaves the device unless you explicitly export an iCalendar (.ics) file or share a Google Calendar link.

2. Permissions the app requests

To deliver loud, on-time trip alarms, the app requests the following Android permissions on your device. None of these grant Holiday Hacker any access to your data; they only let the app do its core job:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — to show alarms when they ring.
  • Schedule exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) — to ring at the exact time you chose, even if the device is in deep sleep.
  • Full-screen intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT) — so the alarm wakes the screen and presents a Dismiss / Snooze UI like a clock app.
  • Foreground service & Wake lock — to play the alarm tone reliably until you dismiss it.
  • Receive boot completed — to re-arm pending alarms after a phone restart so you don't lose them.
  • Vibrate — for the alarm vibration pattern.
  • Internet — only used by the WebView for any third-party links you tap (e.g., Google Calendar render?action=TEMPLATE URLs); the app's core data ships inside the APK and works fully offline.

3. Data sharing

We do not share any data with anyone, because there is no data on our side to share. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or marketing SDKs.

4. Data retention

Data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. If you uninstall the app, Android removes its local storage and all your trip data is permanently deleted. There is no cloud backup or sync.

5. Children's privacy

Holiday Hacker is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any data from children — or anyone.

6. Security

Because data does not leave your device, the security of your trip data reduces to the security of your device itself. We recommend keeping Android up to date and using a secure screen lock.

7. Changes to this policy

If our practices change in any way (for example, we add an opt-in cloud backup), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date at the top will change. The same page is also linked from inside the app.
