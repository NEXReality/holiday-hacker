Disclaimer & Contact

Holiday Hacker is a personal planning assistant that helps you discover Free Holidays, Golden Bridges and Mega Bridges around public and regional holidays, plan destinations to use those windows, and set device reminders for leave application and ticket booking. It runs entirely on your device — there is no account, no cloud sync and no server-side processing of your data.

Information accuracy

Public holiday lists, regional gazettes, destination details and recommendations bundled with this app are compiled from publicly available sources and may contain errors, omissions, or be partially outdated by the time you use them. Holiday dates for some regions (especially festivals tied to lunar calendars) may shift; banks, employers and schools sometimes follow different lists. Destination descriptions, weather windows, permit requirements and travel-mode data are best-effort and not authoritative.

Always verify holiday dates with your employer’s HR list, and travel details with the relevant government, transport or hotel websites, before booking tickets or applying for leave.

Recommendations & scoring

Destination suggestions and the ranking shown in Plan are produced by a heuristic scoring engine using your stated travel modes, climate windows, family profile and preferences. They are guidance, not advice; the app cannot guarantee suitability, safety, availability or value for money of any place suggested. Always do your own research before committing to a trip.

Reminders & alarms

Leave-application reminders, booking heads-up notifications (e.g. the 10-minute IRCTC alarm) and the 65-day holiday-planning notification rely entirely on the Android operating system’s AlarmManager and notification channels. They can be delayed, deferred, silenced or dropped by any of the following, none of which Holiday Hacker can override:

  • Battery optimization / Doze mode (especially on Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO, Realme, Oppo, Vivo / iQOO, Huawei / Honor and similar OEM skins).
  • Do Not Disturb, Focus modes, silent profile or the alarm-stream volume being low or muted.
  • The app being force-stopped, uninstalled, cleared from recent apps aggressively, or having its data cleared.
  • Device reboot before the scheduled time if exact-alarm or boot-completed permissions are revoked.
  • System time / time-zone changes, daylight-saving transitions, or the device being switched off.
  • Future Android updates that further tighten background limits.

Do not rely on Holiday Hacker as your only reminder for time-critical activities such as ticket booking or leave deadlines. Always set a backup alarm in your phone’s native Clock or Calendar app for anything you cannot afford to miss.

Scope of the app

Holiday Hacker is intended solely as a personal assistant to help you (a) discover potential holiday windows around public / regional holidays, (b) plan candidate destinations for those windows, and (c) receive on-device reminders for leave applications and ticket bookings. It is not a travel agent, booking platform, ticket vendor, government holiday register, legal advisor, tax advisor, or financial planner. Treat it as a starting point — then verify everything that materially matters to you with the appropriate authoritative source.

Your data

All of your profile, preferences, selected bridges, planned trips, favorites, custom holidays and reminder settings are stored locally on your device (in browser-storage and the native alarm scheduler). They are not transmitted to any server by this app. App updates from the Play Store will keep this data; uninstalling, clearing app data from Android settings, or tapping “Clear all data & reset app” below will erase them permanently and is not recoverable.
